Accessibility Statement

RGAA 4.1.2, European Standard EN 301 549, and WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance declaration.

Commitment and Scope

RollerCoaster is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities, in compliance with Article 47 of French Law No. 2005-102 of February 11, 2005, European Standard EN 301 549, and WCAG 2.1 Level AA recommendations.

To achieve this, RollerCoaster implements a comprehensive accessibility strategy: a multi-year accessibility plan (2026–2028) and an ongoing annual action plan (2026).

This declaration applies to the Business Intelligence and analytical modeling workspace RollerCoaster.

Compliance Status

RollerCoaster (app.rollercoaster.com) is partially compliant (partiellement conforme) with the General Accessibility Improvement Framework (RGAA version 4.1.2), European standard EN 301 549 V2.1.2, and WCAG 2.1 Level AA recommendations, due to the non-compliances listed below.

Test Results

The compliance audit conducted internally across RGAA 4.1.2 and WCAG 2.1 AA criteria indicates:

  • 78% of RGAA version 4.1.2 criteria are satisfied.
  • The average online service compliance rate is 84%.

Non-Accessible Content

Non-Compliance

  • Criteria 3.2 and 3.3 (Color Contrast): Continuous remediation of custom chart palettes and interactive status controls in light theme to enforce strict contrast thresholds (≥ 4.5:1 for normal text, ≥ 3.0:1 for graphical UI objects).
  • Criteria 1.3 and 4.1 (Text Alternatives for Canvas Visualizations): Finalization of structured, accessible HTML data table alternatives for each dynamic Chart.js canvas visualization.
  • Criteria 12.6 and 7.1 (Keyboard Navigation and Focus Management): Ongoing optimization of sequential focus trapping and restoration inside R-Query drawers and collapsible dock sections.

Disproportionate Burden Derogations

No disproportionate burden derogations are currently invoked.

Exempted Content

No content is exempted from the accessibility requirements.

Multi-Year Accessibility Plan

In accordance with Article 47 of Law No. 2005-102 and RGAA 4.1.2 Section 1.8.2, our multi-year framework and annual action plan ensure:

  1. Multi-Year Plan (2026–2028): Systematic automated execution of axe-core and Zoneless Vitest accessibility suites on every Pull Request, continuous developer training on WCAG/RGAA guidelines, and recurring annual compliance evaluations.
  2. 2026 Annual Action Plan (Q1–Q4): Automated non-regression accessibility gates in CI/CD, theme-neutral graphical palette enforcement, and full audit of RSDL semantic modeling surfaces.
