Privacy Policy

General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation EU 2016/679) compliance and data sovereignty policies.

1. Secure Self-Hosted Infrastructure

All data processing workflows and platform databases are hosted on secured, private infrastructure adhering to the strict requirements of data protection regulations.

2. Zero Telemetry and Local Assets

  • Zero Telemetry: No telemetry, tracking beacons, or behavioral usage data are transmitted to third-party providers.
  • Local Resources: Typography fonts, stylesheets, and geographical map tiles are served directly from the platform infrastructure without external CDN calls.

3. Data Protection and Sensitive Attributes Masking

Sensitive attributes within data models are dynamically masked in visual outputs. The platform operates without external or third-party AI processing services; operational business transactions and confidential row data are never transmitted offsite.
